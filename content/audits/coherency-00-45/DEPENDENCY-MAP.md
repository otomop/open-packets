# Course dependency map

This compact map follows all 46 chapters in order. It summarizes the four chapter-review reports and coverage ledgers; the [audit overview](README.md) links the complete evidence. Entry references are selected actual earlier teaching anchors. The teaching and practice columns describe the principal progression, not every section or exercise.

Links in the entry column open the source lesson; their labels give the chapter number and stable course section ID for precise lookup. Every reference resolves to an earlier chapter. The full coverage ledgers contain all recorded entry and local teaching anchors, including the broader foundations for the two final chapters.

**Next use** lists the earliest later chapters whose coverage records explicitly cite this chapter as an entry owner, up to three examples. It is not an exhaustive dependency graph or a promise that a later lab exercises every topic. Short explained previews and optional exercises are documented separately in the range reviews.
