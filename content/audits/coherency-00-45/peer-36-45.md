## Specific boundary evidence

- `nat-pat / dynamic-nat-with-a-pool` and `verify-and-troubleshoot` teach source selection, nonselection and actual-source testing. The capstone's `hq-and-the-internet-edge` accurately applies those rules and retains upstream return-route assumptions.
- `dhcp / the-four-messages-dora` explains the initial zero source and UDP client/server roles. The capstone's `policy-and-management` therefore has a taught basis for permitting initial DHCP before source-subnet rules.
- `acls / extended-acls` and `applying-an-acl-interfaces-direction-and-the-vty-lines` teach ICMP replies as separate packets, interface direction, stateless TCP flags and separate VTY admission. The capstone's guest policy does not promise general stateful return permission or same-VLAN isolation.
- `file-management / tftp-ftp-scp-and-sftp` and its secure-transfer lab teach trusted host-key verification before readiness q24 and remote Ansible identity checks. Successful login, file access and verified transfer remain distinct stages.
- `layer2-security / dynamic-arp-inspection-arp-must-match-the-bindings` now includes the authorized static-printer IP/MAC ACL, explicit deny and nonmatch fallback, supporting readiness q28 without trusting every client.
- Readiness q21 can reuse 35's endpoint/mode diagrams; its interpretation does not depend on completing optional advanced VPN configuration.
