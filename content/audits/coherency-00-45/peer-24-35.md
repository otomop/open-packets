The final `four-parameters-decide-everything` section now defines a server certificate, expected identity, trust, validity period, and why a wrong clock can break the check. This is sufficient for the client diagnosis without moving the full security chapter. **Resolved and reread.**

2. **Chapter 29 question 21 retained an unsupported explanation clause.** The corrected main explanation properly identifies current running-config, SCP, off-device storage, and preservation of startup-config. However, `whyWrong["3"]` still claimed a local save necessarily overwrote the recovery checkpoint, while the corresponding option only said “saved running-config locally.” A local flash snapshot would not imply that overwrite. Sent a narrow explanation-only correction: the wrong off-device source is already sufficient to reject that option. Root applied the correction centrally; the final text no longer infers which local store was changed. **Resolved and reread; options, answer and item ID preserved.**

No additional unresolved knowledge-order gap was found in the reviewed practice demands. Those bridges are preferable here to moving whole chapters or treating later material as an undeclared prerequisite.
